Top 5 Cyber Threats in Kenya 2026

Published July 2026 • 6 min read

Kenyan businesses are increasingly under attack. Here are the five biggest cyber threats you must prepare for in 2026:

1. Phishing & Social Engineering

Still the number one entry point. Attackers target employees with realistic emails, WhatsApp messages, and even voice calls (vishing).

2. Ransomware

Especially targeting SMEs that lack proper backups. Once encrypted, recovery without paying is extremely difficult.

3. Business Email Compromise (BEC)

Fraudsters impersonate CEOs or suppliers and trick finance teams into making payments.

4. Weak Password & Credential Stuffing

Many Kenyan companies still reuse passwords across systems. One breach leads to many.

5. Unpatched Software & Outdated Systems

Legacy systems and delayed updates remain a major vulnerability, especially in the public and SME sectors.
